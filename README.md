# E.English

**E.English** is a free vocabulary study website for learning English. It works offline after the first visit and keeps all your progress on your own device. There are no accounts and no sign-up.

> **Version 11.3.1.** Open a lesson, mark the words you have learned, take a quiz, then come back to the words you missed.

## What you can do

- Study vocabulary, synonyms and antonyms, idioms and phrasal verbs, and derivatives, organized into **units** and **lessons** (currently six units).
- Mark words and whole groups of words as **learned**.
- Take a **Full Quiz** or a **Selected Quiz** and see detailed results.
- Keep a **Review** list of words that need more work.
- Follow your **Statistics** and your **Quiz History**.
- **Search** any word and jump straight to it.
- Use the app in **English or Arabic** (Arabic is shown right-to-left), in light or dark mode, with seven accent colors.
- Study **offline**, back up and restore your progress, and send **feedback**.

## Units and lessons

The Home screen lists the units. Each unit card shows the **total number of words** in the unit (vocabulary, synonyms and antonyms, idioms and phrasal verbs, and derivatives together) and how many of them you have learned, plus a progress bar for the whole unit. The same totals are used in Statistics. They come from the lesson content itself, so they update when the lessons change.

Inside a lesson, each content type is its own section, and words are arranged in small groups you can open and close. Each word shows its type, difficulty and Arabic meaning. Synonyms and Antonyms use larger study cards.

## Parts of speech

Many words carry a small colored tag such as `n.` (noun), `v.` (verb) or `adj.` (adjective) that shows what job the word does in a sentence. A word with two uses has two tags, such as `n./v.`. The **Info** page explains every tag.

## Quizzes

Quizzes open from the **Vocabulary** section of a lesson and cover that lesson only.

| Mode | Words used |
| --- | --- |
| **Full Quiz** | Every vocabulary word in the lesson. |
| **Selected Quiz** | Only the words you marked as learned. It unlocks as soon as one word is marked. |

- Each button shows an **estimated time** and the number of words. The real time limit is more generous than the estimate.
- A quiz takes the whole screen. You see the Arabic meaning and type the English word. Use **Previous** and **Next** (or Enter) to move, change answers any time, and jump to any question from the question bar.
- A question left empty counts as **skipped**, and you can still go back to it.
- Nothing is marked right or wrong while you answer. Answers are checked only when you finish, or when time runs out.
- A running timer turns amber, then red, as time runs low.

**Scoring:** a correct answer scores 1 point, an answer that is close to correct (a small typing mistake) scores 0.5, and wrong or skipped answers score 0. The result screen shows your score, percentage and the number of correct, close, wrong and skipped answers, with full details for every question under **Advanced Details**.

## Review

A word is in Review when you flag it yourself with the **Review** button on its card, or when you get it **wrong** or **skip** it in a quiz. A close answer does not add a word, and marking a word as learned clears an entry that came from a quiz (not a flag you set yourself).

On the Statistics page the Review list is **grouped by unit**. Each word is shown in English with its Arabic translation, and tapping it opens the word in its lesson. Each item has the same **flag** button as the word cards. Tap it to remove the word from Review: the item stays visible for 3 seconds, and you can tap the flag again to keep it. After that it leaves the list. Removing a word from Review does not delete the word or change your learned words or quiz history.

## Statistics and Quiz History

- **Statistics** shows overall progress, progress for each content type, and the Review list.
- **Quiz History** keeps your last 40 quizzes, newest first. Open one to see the same detailed result screen. Each entry is a snapshot, so an old result stays the same even if lessons change later.

## Search

Type in the search box on Home to find any word or meaning. Pick a result to open it in its lesson.

## Settings

- **Language:** English or Arabic. The whole interface changes, including the layout direction. Lesson content (words, meanings, the Arabic study text) is not translated.
- **Theme mode:** Light, Dark or System. Three round buttons sit together: **Keep screen awake**, **theme** and **language**. They are at the top of the screen on phones and tablets, and beside the navigation bar on desktop, and they are hidden during a quiz. The Keep screen awake button and the switch in Settings always show the same state.
- **Font style:** **Auto** (the recommended default: bold headings, regular text), **Regular** or **Bold**.
- **Accent color:** Default (neutral graphite), Burgundy, Lavender, Sky Blue, Lime, Orange or Teal, shown as named options, in both Light and Dark. The chosen palette colors the whole app (cards, tags, numbered steps, progress), while success, warning, error and the Review flag keep their own colors. Success, warning, error and the Review flag keep their own colors. If an earlier version saved a palette that is no longer offered, it keeps working until you choose a new one.
- **Animations:** switch movement on or off, with a small preview. If your device asks for reduced motion, animations stay off.
- **Local storage:** shows how much browser storage the site uses. It is a real measurement from your browser when your browser provides it; otherwise it says the value is unavailable. The bar uses a 1 GB reference scale only for scale, and browsers do not all guarantee 1 GB.
- **Keep screen awake:** stops the screen from turning off while you study, where your browser supports it.
- **Data management** (the last section of the page, always visible): **Export Data**, **Import Data** and **Delete All Data**.

## Info and Feedback

- **Info** shows the app version, six feature tags, a three-sentence description, what is new in this version, the Study Method, the parts of speech, and the ownership notice.
- **Feedback** explains how to report a bug, suggest an improvement or share an opinion, and has a **Contact me on WhatsApp** button that opens a chat with the developer (`@Ma7mee7o`). The website does not send, collect or store your message.

## Working offline and updates

After your first visit with internet, the app and all lessons are saved in your browser, so lessons, search, quizzes, Statistics, Review and Settings work without a connection. When a new version is available, the app refreshes its own files once the new ones are ready, and never in the middle of a quiz. Updates replace app files only; your learning data is never touched.

## Your data and privacy

- Your progress (learned words, Review list, quiz history, settings) is saved only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no tracking.
- The app makes no network requests for normal studying. The only link that leaves the app is the WhatsApp button, and only when you tap it.
- Clearing the site's data in your browser, or **Delete All Data**, removes your progress for good unless you exported a backup.
- **Export Data** saves a backup file; **Import Data** replaces your current progress with the file's contents after you confirm.

## Devices and limits

- Works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on phones, tablets and computers. The navigation is a floating bar; when the items do not fit the screen width, the extra ones move into a **More** menu.
- Progress belongs to one browser on one device. Use Export and Import to move it.
- A quiz is not saved halfway: if you leave, its answers are discarded.
- Quizzes cover one lesson pair at a time and use only vocabulary words.
- Renaming or moving a word inside the lessons makes it a new word, and its old progress is dropped.
- The first visit needs internet so the lessons can be saved for offline use.
